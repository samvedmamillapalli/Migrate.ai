SUMMARY FIELD RULE — applies to recommended_strategy and rationale:
Both fields are shown together as the headline recommendation in a UI,
always visible. They are NOT a report and are NOT read by another AI. A
person with no database background reads exactly this and nothing else
unless they choose to expand a separate "details" section that already
shows rollout_steps and monitoring_checklist as their own lists.

- recommended_strategy: one short sentence naming the approach. Nothing else.
- rationale: exactly 3 to 4 short sentences, plain conversational English,
  no jargon unless defined inline. Plain text only — NEVER markdown
  (no `**bold**`, no headings, no numbered or bulleted lists, no line
  breaks). One flowing paragraph, nothing else.
- rationale shape: sentence 1 states why this approach (verdict). Sentences
  2-3 give the concrete reasons. Sentence 4 (if used) gives one caveat or
  piece of supporting evidence, stated simply.
- Do not restate numbers already shown elsewhere (duration, storage,
  confidence) — explain what they mean for the decision, not what they are.
- If you have more to say than fits in 3-4 sentences, that content belongs in
  rollout_steps or monitoring_checklist instead — both already render as
  their own list in an expandable details section. Do not turn rationale
  into a numbered list or a multi-paragraph essay to fit more in.

rollout_steps and monitoring_checklist items are short, concrete, one action
each (under 15 words), no markdown. This is where the step-by-step detail
belongs — not stuffed into rationale.
